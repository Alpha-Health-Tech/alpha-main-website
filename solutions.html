Bookings & patient engagement

Let patients book themselves in.

Phone-only booking loses patients. We set up online booking for the services you actually run — travel vaccinations, flu jabs, consultations, health checks — and connect it to reminders so fewer appointments are missed.

  • Online booking calendars for clinics and consultation rooms
  • Email and SMS confirmations and appointment reminders
  • Repeat prescription request forms that reach the right inbox
  • Newsletter sign-up and patient contact lists, handled with consent in mind

Integrations & reporting

Your tools, joined up.

Most pharmacies already use a handful of systems that do not talk to each other. We connect what can sensibly be connected and give you plain-English reporting on what your website is actually doing.

Booking, form and enquiry data flows into the tools you already use, your Google Business Profile stays consistent with your site, and analytics are set up properly with cookie consent handled from the start.

You then get a periodic report in plain English — visits, bookings, enquiries, calls — rather than a dashboard you never open. And when an off-the-shelf tool is the better answer, we say so, even when it is not ours to sell.

We build it, we host it, and we answer the phone when something breaks.

Questions

The practical details.

what owners usually ask us

How quickly do you respond to problems?

We reply within one business day, Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:30pm. Urgent issues such as a site being down go to the front of the queue.

What exactly does a care plan include?

Hosting, SSL, software updates, backups, security monitoring and small content changes. Larger pieces of work — a new service page, a redesign — are quoted separately and fixed before we start.

Can you take over a site you did not build?

Usually, yes. We will review it first and tell you honestly whether it is worth maintaining or better rebuilt — and what each would cost.

Are we locked in?

No. You own your domain, your content and your site. If you ever move on, we hand everything over cleanly.
