Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 March 2026

1. Introduction

Alpha Health Tech Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services, including our social media management platform and any associated applications.

We are registered in England and Wales. Our registered office is at Alpha Health Tech, United Kingdom.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Name and email address when you contact us or create an account
  • Social media account credentials and access tokens when you connect your accounts (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn)
  • Content you create, upload, or publish through our platform (text, images, videos)
  • Business information such as company name, website URL, and branding details

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Log data including IP address, browser type, and access times
  • Usage data about how you interact with our services
  • Device information including operating system and device identifiers

2.3 Information from third parties

  • Social media profile information when you connect your accounts via OAuth (name, profile picture, page/organization details)
  • Website content when you submit URLs for analysis (publicly available information only)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our services
  • Generate social media content and post it to your connected accounts on your behalf
  • Schedule and manage your social media posts
  • Analyse website content to generate relevant post ideas
  • Improve and develop our services
  • Communicate with you about updates, support, and service-related matters
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Social Media Data

When you connect your social media accounts, we access and store:

  • Facebook/Instagram: Page access tokens, page IDs, page names, and Instagram business account details. We use these to publish content to your pages on your behalf.
  • LinkedIn: Access tokens, organization IDs, and organization names. We use these to publish content to your company pages on your behalf.

We do not access your personal messages, friend lists, or private content. We only request the minimum permissions necessary to post content on your behalf.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers: Third-party services that help us operate our platform (e.g., hosting providers, AI services for content generation)
  • Social media platforms: When you authorise us to post content on your behalf
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. Social media access tokens are stored for the duration of their validity period. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 8).

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate personal data
  • Request erasure of your personal data
  • Restrict or object to processing of your data
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@alphahealthtech.co.uk.

You can also request deletion of your user data through our User Data Deletion page.

9. Cookies

Our services may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to maintain sessions and improve user experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: info@alphahealthtech.co.uk
  • Website: alphahealthtech.co.uk
