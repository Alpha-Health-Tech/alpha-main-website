Questions

Honest answers before you commit.

the questions everyone asks first

How long does a pharmacy website take?

From the intro call to launch, expect ten days to a month at most. The biggest variable is content: if you can review pages promptly and send us your service details, we stay at the fast end of that range.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope — a bookings-led clinic site is more work than a straightforward pharmacy site — so we quote after the intro call. The quote is fixed: one written price, no hourly billing, and no surprises on the invoice.

Can you rework our current site instead of starting again?

Sometimes. If your current site has a sound structure we can rework the design and content on top of it. Often a rebuild is cheaper than untangling an old platform — we will tell you honestly which applies after we have looked at it.

Do we own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and the site, its content and its design belong to your pharmacy. If you ever move on from us, everything transfers with you.

Who writes the content?

We do the heavy lifting. We draft your service pages from what you tell us on the call and from your existing material, then you review and correct them — you check the clinical detail, we handle the writing.
